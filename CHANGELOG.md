# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- **Dyed Resin Clumps** in all sixteen dye colours: one clump and a dye, or eight clumps around a dye.
- Placed, broken and dropped like vanilla's resin clump; a dyed clump takes any other dye.
- **Washing out**: a dyed clump alone in the crafting grid turns plain again.
- Guide book in English and German.
